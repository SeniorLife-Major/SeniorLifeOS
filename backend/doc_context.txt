  New document types

  1. death_certificate (मृत्यु प्रमाण पत्र, issued under the Registration of Births
  and Deaths Act)
  - Required: deceased's name, date of death, date issued, issuing authority.
  - Optional: "name of husband/wife" (spouse_name), age at death, place of
    death, registration number.
  - Checks:
    - The date of death can't be in the future, or after the date issued.
    - The spouse name is compared with the owner's name, allowing for small
      spelling differences.
  - Design point: the certificate belongs to the widow but is about another
    person. Its name field must not be called name. Otherwise the existing
    checks would report "name differs from the owner" and "your documents have
    different names" and block her.
  - Not health data: the civil certificate has no cause of death. The doctor's
    cause-of-death form is a separate paper and isn't accepted as this type.

  2. ration_card (proof of BPL)
  - Required: head of family, card category (AAY / PHH / BPL / APL…), state.
  - Optional: family members as a list (name, relation, age), card number, date
    issued.
  - Whether PHH counts as BPL is decided per state, so it goes in each scheme's
    rule data, not in code.

  3. identity gets an optional gender field. Aadhaar prints it, and widow
  schemes need it.

  Later, only if your state asks for it: a widow / not-remarried certificate,
  and a pension payment order (PPO) for government family pensions.

  The rules gain an in comparison (for example, card category is one of AAY,
  BPL, PHH). National schemes (marked IN) apply in every state. Some facts can't
  come from a paper, such as "not remarried" or "no other pension". Those are
  asked, shown as "you told us", and the result stays "likely" rather than
  "eligible".

  Your example: Kamla Devi is 68, lives in Maharashtra, and her vault holds an
  Aadhaar, a ration card and a passbook.
  - Screening shows the widow pension: age ✔, woman ✔, BPL ✔, widowed = unknown,

  - If it reads "Kamala Devi", she's asked to check it instead of it being
    accepted automatically.
  - If the certificate has no spouse name, she's asked to confirm the marriage,
    or to add a widow certificate where the state needs one.
